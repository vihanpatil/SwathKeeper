## The three changes between the takes

1. **Bar 3b joined truth at the wrong instant.** The 2026-11-10 batch read a range error p95 of 2.6 m (min 0.03 m) on every
   run: the checker took the bird's true position at the executor tick that consumed the detection, while the depth frame had
   been rendered 0.1–0.3 s earlier and the bird closes at ~10 m/s. Before take 1, `_log_detection` started writing the frame's
   `stamp_s`, and the gate joins a stamped event at its frame instant with the drone position interpolated to it. Take 1 read
   **0.2051 m** frame-joined; the same log reads 2.0773 m tick-joined. (The 2.6 m figure belongs to the batch, not to take 1.)
2. **Bars 4 and 5 used course over ground for yaw.** Take 1: 14 of 26 detections "behind the camera" during the sidestep,
   while the same frames ranged the bird to 0.05–0.21 m. Before take 2 the executor started writing `flown_heading_rad` (one yaw
   per vertex); bars 4 and 5 read the logged yaw at the frame instant. Take 2: 30 of 30 inside the frustum. Roll and pitch are
   still unlogged; level flight is assumed around the yaw.
3. **Undriven world birds were refused, not scored.** The head-on scenario drives bird_0; bird_1 and bird_2 are the `<static>`
   models at their spawn poses. Take 1 was refused for this as well as for bar 4; take 2 for this alone. Before take 3 the
   checker learned to read the driver's sidecar declaration (config name + bird set) and, when the named config exists, is not the
   world config, its bird set equals the sidecar's and the landed set, and that set is a proper subset of the world's birds, to
   score the undriven birds at spawn as static obstacles. **This was a gate-rule change, not an artifact change**: the sidecars of
   takes 1 and 2 already carried the same declaration, and take 2 re-scores VALID under it. Take 3 then scored VALID at flight
   time. The accepted take is the third attempt, with gate rules changed between attempts — the pre-registration's forking-paths
   caveat applies in full.

## The QA round after take 3 (27 confirmed findings, same day)

The adversarial review of the three changes and of this record found, and the committed checker now does:
- the frame stamp is **checked, not trusted**: an age outside [−0.5 s, `max_detection_age_s`] against the consuming tick is a
  problem and the event is scored at the tick; a detection the truth cannot answer is a **problem**, never a smaller denominator;
- **frames, not consumption events, are the samples** (the executor re-logs a frame on 1–2 ticks: take 3's 29 events are 18
  frames), and frames inside a `set_pose` bracket — where the truth offers two positions a teleport step apart — are **reported,
  not gated**: scoring the nearer candidate let the bracket buy accuracy, scoring the farther one made every take read 2.1–2.3 m
  of bird teleport; the gated p95 runs over the unambiguous frames (7 of 18 on take 3), and a take with none is unmeasured;
- a **NaN yaw is refused** (it had compared "inside" every half-angle), the yaw list is validated against the path, and the
  booking pin carries the artifact's **sha256** and compares resolved paths;
- the freeze debit takes the **faster of the world's and the declared scenario's** birds; a declared-static bird is held to the
  3.00 m bar over the **whole flight** (3D), including after the driver's last landed call;
- the published numbers were corrected (take 3's runtime had been quoted with take 2's; cross-course ranges; the 2.6 m before/after
  framing; "29 detections" → 18 frames), the scenario fixtures were regenerated (stale since ADR-013 am. 20; their open-loop paths
  now end in GUIDED with no resume — a declared coverage gap in `eval/scenarios/README.md`), and the pre-registration got its
  dated OUTCOME sections.

## What this is, and is not

One head-on clearance at 5.0 m/s on a forward depth camera, in sim, against a teleported bird with no collision geometry,
**VALID under the flight-log gate** with every pre-registered depth bar measured — its am. 5 booking precondition measured and
closed on 2026-11-12, after the flight rather than before it, §0b ruled reported-only the same day — as the third of three attempts with gate rules
changed between them. The offline gate is the entire safety system. It
does not retire the three breaches of August, the 2026-08-25 INVALID take or the declared CI red; it does not make the drone a
product; it says nothing about geometries a forward camera cannot see (astern overtake, arrival mid U-turn). The next evidence
step is a booked batch under the same gate.
