## The three gaps, and what closed them

1. **Bar 3b joined truth at the wrong instant.** The 2026-11-10 batch read a range error p95 of 2.6 m (min 0.03 m) on every
   run: the checker took the bird's true position at the executor tick that consumed the detection, while the depth frame had
   been rendered 0.1–0.4 s earlier and the bird closes at ~10 m/s. `_log_detection` now writes the frame's `stamp_s`; the gate
   joins a stamped event at its frame instant with the drone position interpolated to it, and says "tick-joined" on a legacy
   log. Take 1 read **0.2051 m** — the first in-flight measurement of the sensor rather than of the clock.
2. **Bar 4 used course over ground for yaw.** Take 1: 14 of 26 detections "behind the camera" during the sidestep, while
   the same frames ranged the bird to 0.05–0.21 m. The executor now writes `flown_heading_rad` (one yaw per vertex); bars 4
   and 5 read the logged yaw at the frame instant. Take 2: 30 of 30 inside the frustum.
3. **Undriven world birds were refused, not scored.** The head-on scenario drives bird_0; bird_1 and bird_2 are the
   `<static>` models at their spawn poses. The driver's sidecar declares the config and bird set it drove, and the checker
   accepts the declaration only if the named config exists here, is not the world config, its bird set equals the sidecar's
   and the landed set, and that set is a proper subset of the world's birds — then it scores the rest at spawn, as static
   obstacles. Anything else stays refused. Take 3: VALID.

## What this is, and is not

One evidence-gated head-on clearance at 5.0 m/s on a forward depth camera, in sim, against a teleported bird with no collision
geometry — the offline gate is the entire safety system. It does not retire the three breaches of August, the 2026-08-25
INVALID take or the declared CI red; it does not make the drone a product; and it says nothing about geometries a forward
camera cannot see (astern overtake, arrival mid U-turn). The next evidence step is a booked batch under the same gate, after
the owner answers the sensor-coverage question.
